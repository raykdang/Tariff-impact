Tariff on clothing from Vietnam: T-shirts, jeans and jackets

Clothing from Vietnam pays two layers of duty on entry to the United States: the ordinary duty for the garment, which is the larger part (16.5% on cotton T-shirts, 16.6% on cotton jeans), and 12.5% on top since 24 July 2026. So a cotton T-shirt pays 29% and cotton jeans 29.1%. Below: the rate for each garment, a T-shirt and a pair of jeans worked through, and how Vietnam compares.

Rates last checked: 25 November 2026. Estimates only, not legal, customs or financial advice.

Ordinary duty: Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, 2026 Revision 23, column 1 general, read 25 November 2026. The added 12.5%: the Section 301 country-wide duty on products of Vietnam, USTR notice, 91 FR 47318, in force for goods entered on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern time on 24 July 2026. Tariff schedule heading 9903.05.84.

Is clothing exempt? No. Of clothing, only worn (used) clothing is in the groups the USTR notice exempts (section III.D). Vietnam is not among the thirteen countries the notice gives an exempt list of their own.

The 2026 revision of the schedule splits these trouser and jacket codes into two parallel sets of lines with the same rates, so the rates above hold whichever set your broker uses. Garments of other fibres (wool, synthetic T-shirts, blends where cotton is not the main fibre) have other codes and rates that we did not read: check yours in the Harmonized Tariff Schedule.

The temporary 10% surcharge of 2026 ended on 24 July 2026, and the 2025 emergency duties ended on 20 February 2026. Neither applies now.

A fee also applies to each shipment: the Merchandise Processing Fee, 0.3464% of the goods' value with a minimum of $34.58 and a maximum of $670.86 per formal entry, from 1 October 2026.

We did not check whether any anti-dumping or countervailing duty applies to your product. A customs broker can tell you.

What it does to a T-shirt and a pair of jeans

A cotton T-shirt: supplier price $3.20, freight $0.25 a unit, sold to shops at $9.00. Cotton jeans: supplier price $9.50, freight $0.60, sold at $28.00. Vietnam against other clothing sources

The ordinary duty on a garment is the same from China, Vietnam, India and Bangladesh. What differs is what comes on top. For a cotton T-shirt:

What clothing importers can do

  • Check fibre and construction. Knitted or woven, cotton or synthetic, water resistant or not: each changes the code and the ordinary duty, sometimes by 20 points.
  • Compare countries on what comes on top. The ordinary duty is the same from these four countries; the added layers are not.
  • Consolidate entries to spread the $34.58 minimum fee and broker charges.
  • Reprice where the margin needs it; see absorb the tariff or raise prices, and the price increase letter templates.

Questions

What is the tariff on clothing from Vietnam?

Ordinary duty for the garment plus 12.5% since 24 July 2026. Cotton T-shirts (6109.10.00) pay 16.5% + 12.5% = 29%; cotton jeans and trousers (6203.42, 6204.62) 16.6% + 12.5% = 29.1%; ordinary synthetic jackets 27.7% + 12.5% = 40.2%. Checked 25 November 2026.

Is clothing exempt from the Vietnam tariff?

No. Of clothing, only worn (used) clothing is exempt from the 2026 country-wide duty, and Vietnam has no exempt list of its own in the USTR notice.

Is it cheaper to import clothing from Vietnam or from China?

From Vietnam, on duty. Both pay 12.5% on top of the same ordinary duty, but Chinese clothing also pays the older list 4A duty of 7.5%. On a $3.20 T-shirt that is 24 cents.
