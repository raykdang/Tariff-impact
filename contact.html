Other ways we can help

I'm a small business owner — can you help me with my specific tariff situation?
We're a free educational resource, not a customs brokerage. For your specific HTS code and product, you'll need to consult a licensed customs broker or trade attorney. That said, if you have feedback about our calculator, the data we publish, or topics we should cover, we'd love to hear it. Email us anytime.
I'm a journalist — can I cite your data or interview you?
Yes. Our tariff rate dataset is freely usable with attribution to Tariff-Impact.com. For interviews, comments, or background on small business tariff impact, email us with your deadline and we'll do our best to respond promptly.
I found a bug or wrong tariff rate — how do I report it?
Email us with the corridor and HTS code, plus your source for the corrected rate. We update the dataset regularly and your feedback genuinely helps. We credit corrections in our update notes.
Do you accept guest posts?
We're selective but open to high-quality guest contributions on small business trade strategy, sourcing case studies, or technical tariff topics. Pitches must include a topic outline and a writing sample. Generic SEO link-building requests will be ignored.

Response time

We aim to respond to all email within 2 business days. Advertising inquiries are usually answered within one business day. If your request is time-sensitive (e.g., a journalist deadline), please note it in the subject line.
