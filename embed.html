What your readers get

  • The tariff rate for a country pair from the same rates table as our full calculator, with the date it was checked, or a rate they type in.
  • Cost per unit and margin per unit before and after the tariff.
  • The change in monthly profit, allowing for the share of the tariff passed on and for customers buying less at a higher price.
  • The price that would keep profit where it was before the tariff.
  • A link that opens their figures in the full calculator, with charts and strategy comparisons.

The sums are the same code as the full calculator, so the two always give the same answer.

The terms, in plain words

  • Estimates only. It is an educational tool, not customs, legal or financial advice. Your readers should confirm their own product's rate in the official tariff schedule.

Questions

Can I open the calculator on a particular country?

Yes. Pick the countries under "Opening countries" above, and the code fills that country pair's rate when the page opens. Your readers can still change it.

Will it slow my page down?

The frame waits until a reader scrolls near it before it loads, and the calculator is one small page with no images or outside scripts.

Do the rates update?

Yes. The frame shows the calculator from our site, so when we update a rate, every copy of the calculator shows the new figure.
