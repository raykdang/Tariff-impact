Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF) calculator

The Merchandise Processing Fee is US Customs’ charge for handling an import entry. From 1 October 2026 it is 0.3464% of the value of the goods, with a minimum of $34.58 and a maximum of $670.86 per formal entry. Enter your shipment’s value to see yours.

How to pay less MPF, within the rules

  • Fewer, larger entries. Two $5,000 shipments pay $34.58 each, $69.16 in all; one $10,000 shipment pays $34.64. Combine orders where lead time allows.
  • Check the value your broker declares. The fee is on the goods’ value. If an invoice includes freight or other charges, ask your broker how they are treated.
  • Ask about trade-deal goods. Some goods that qualify under a trade agreement may be treated differently. We did not check which for this page: your broker can say.

Small, low-value shipments may be entered informally, and informal entries pay a different, fixed-amount fee. We have not checked those figures for this page, so the calculator covers formal entries only.

Where the MPF sits in your costs

The fee is one line of your landed cost, next to the duty, freight, broker and bond. On our worked China shipment of $6,400 it is the $34.58 minimum, 2 cents a shirt. For how it differs from duty and other charges, see tariff vs duty vs customs fees. To add it to the full calculator, divide it by your units and put it in “other variable cost”.

Questions

What is the Merchandise Processing Fee?

A fee US Customs and Border Protection charges on imports to cover the cost of processing them. It is paid with the duty on each entry. On a formal entry it is a percentage of the value of the goods, with a floor and a ceiling.

How much is the MPF in 2027?

For fiscal year 2027, from 1 October 2026, the fee on a formal entry is 0.3464% of the value of the goods, at least $34.58 and at most $670.86. CBP set these figures in CBP Dec. 26-14, published in the Federal Register on 31 July 2026 (91 FR 48398).

When does the MPF minimum or maximum apply?

The minimum of $34.58 applies to any formal entry valued under about $9,983, and the maximum of $670.86 to any entry valued over about $193,666. Between those values the fee is 0.3464% of the value: $86.60 on $25,000, for example.

Is the MPF the same as duty?

No. Duty is a tax on the goods, set by the tariff code and the country of origin. The MPF is a processing fee charged on the entry whatever the goods are. Both are paid to Customs on the same entry, usually by your broker.
