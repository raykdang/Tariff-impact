Where the tariff sits in an FBA unit cost

The tariff is charged once, when the goods enter the United States, on the value of the goods: the supplier’s price, not your Amazon price and not the freight. So it belongs in your landed cost, next to freight and prep, before any Amazon fee. Amazon’s own fees don’t change because of it.

What changes is the price you need. Amazon’s referral fee is a share of the price, so every dollar you add to the price brings you only 85 cents at a 15% fee. To pass on a tariff of $T a unit in full, the price must rise by T ÷ (1 − referral rate): $0.75 ÷ 0.85 = $0.88 (0.882). The calculator rounds the new price up to the next cent so your profit is never short, which is why it shows $30.88, a rise of $0.89.

When to cut the product

  • Profit after the tariff is below what the stock costs to hold. Long-term storage fees and slow sales can wipe out a thin margin.
  • The price that keeps your profit loses you the Buy Box, and the price that keeps the Buy Box loses money after ads.
  • A cheaper source exists. The same product from another country may pay less. See China vs Vietnam vs Mexico vs India and toys from Vietnam.
  • The tariff is about to change. Check what changes in January 2027 before you place the next order.

Questions

How do I add a tariff to my Amazon FBA costs?

Multiply the price you pay the supplier by the tariff rate, and add the result to your unit cost next to freight and prep. On a $6.00 toy from China at 12.5%, that is $0.75 a unit. Amazon’s fees do not change because of the tariff, but your profit per unit falls by the full $0.75.

How much do I need to raise my Amazon price to cover a tariff?

More than the tariff, because Amazon’s referral fee is a share of the price. To keep the same profit per unit, divide the tariff per unit by one minus your referral fee rate. With a 15% referral fee, a $0.75 tariff needs a rise of $0.88 ($0.75 ÷ 0.85 = $0.882); with the price rounded up to the next cent, $29.99 becomes $30.88.

What is the tariff on toys from China for Amazon sellers?

12.5% in total since 24 July 2026: the ordinary duty on toys is Free, no older China list duty reaches heading 9503.00.00 today, and the 12.5% country-wide duty applies. Checked 25 November 2026. Other products have other rates.

Who pays the tariff when I ship to an Amazon warehouse?

The importer of record: the business named on the customs entry. For most FBA sellers that is you, through your freight forwarder or broker, unless your supplier ships DDP and pays the duty itself, in which case the duty is inside the supplier’s price.

How the figures were worked out

Profit per unit = price − supplier price − tariff − freight − referral fee rate × price − fulfilment fee − storage − advertising. Price to keep profit = (old profit + all costs except the referral fee) ÷ (1 − referral rate). The full calculator’s figures use the same costs, with the referral fee fixed at its $29.99 value ($12.40 of Amazon fees and ads in all).
