Tariff on clothing from China: T-shirts, jeans and jackets

Clothing from China pays three layers of duty on entry to the United States: the ordinary duty for the garment (16.5% on cotton T-shirts, 16.6% on cotton jeans), the older China duty on list 4A (+7.5%), and the 2026 country-wide duty of 12.5%. So a cotton T-shirt pays 36.5% and cotton jeans 36.6%. Below: the rate for each garment, a T-shirt and a pair of jeans worked through, and how China compares with Vietnam, India and Bangladesh.

Rates last checked: 25 November 2026. Estimates only, not legal, customs or financial advice.

Ordinary duty: Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, 2026 Revision 23, column 1 general, read 25 November 2026. The older China duty: every code in the table is on list 4A, +7.5% (heading 9903.88.15, US note 20(s)(i)); none is on lists 1 to 3, none has a 2024 increase (US note 31), and we found no exclusion for them. USTR confirmed on 7 October 2026 that the older China duties remain in effect. The 12.5%: the Section 301 country-wide duty on products of China, USTR notice, 91 FR 47318, heading 9903.05.31, in force for goods entered on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern time on 24 July 2026. The three layers stack.

One exception we can't settle: women's padded sleeveless synthetic jackets (6202.40.35, ordinary duty 14.9%) are missing from the list 4A table as the schedule prints it, though the men's version (6201.40.35) is on it, at 14.9% + 7.5% + 12.5% = 34.9%. We give no total for the women's line: ask your broker.

Is clothing exempt? No. Of clothing, only worn (used) clothing is in the groups the USTR notice exempts (section III.D). China is not among the thirteen countries the notice gives an exempt list of their own. The 10% China duty of November 2025 (heading 9903.01.24) ended on 20 February 2026 and does not come on top.

The 2026 revision of the schedule splits these trouser and jacket codes into two parallel sets of lines with the same rates, so the rates above hold whichever set your broker uses. Garments of other fibres (wool, synthetic T-shirts, blends where cotton is not the main fibre) have other codes and rates that we did not read: check yours in the Harmonized Tariff Schedule.

The temporary 10% surcharge of 2026 ended on 24 July 2026, and the 2025 emergency duties ended on 20 February 2026. Neither applies now.

A fee also applies to each shipment: the Merchandise Processing Fee, 0.3464% of the goods' value with a minimum of $34.58 and a maximum of $670.86 per formal entry, from 1 October 2026.

We did not check whether any anti-dumping or countervailing duty applies to your product. A customs broker can tell you.

What it does to a T-shirt and a pair of jeans

A cotton T-shirt: supplier price $3.20, freight $0.25 a unit, sold to shops at $9.00. Cotton jeans: supplier price $9.50, freight $0.60, sold at $28.00. China against other clothing sources

The ordinary duty on a garment is the same from China, Vietnam, India and Bangladesh. What differs is what comes on top. For a cotton T-shirt:

What clothing importers can do

  • Check fibre and construction. Knitted or woven, cotton or synthetic, water resistant or not: each changes the code and the ordinary duty, sometimes by 20 points.
  • Compare countries on what comes on top. The ordinary duty is the same from these four countries; the added layers are not.
  • Consolidate entries to spread the $34.58 minimum fee and broker charges.
  • Reprice where the margin needs it; see absorb the tariff or raise prices, and the price increase letter templates.

Questions

What is the tariff on clothing from China?

Three layers: ordinary duty for the garment, the older China list 4A duty of 7.5%, and the 2026 country-wide duty of 12.5%. Cotton T-shirts (6109.10.00) pay 16.5% + 7.5% + 12.5% = 36.5%; cotton jeans and trousers 36.6%; ordinary synthetic jackets 47.7%; water-resistant ones 27.1%. Checked 25 November 2026.

Which Section 301 list is clothing from China on?

List 4A, +7.5% (heading 9903.88.15). Cotton T-shirts, cotton trousers and synthetic jackets are all on it, and on no list 1 to 3 or 2024 increase. One line, women's padded sleeveless synthetic jackets (6202.40.35), is missing from the printed table.

Does the 10% China tariff apply to clothing?

No. The 10% China duty of November 2025 (heading 9903.01.24) ended on 20 February 2026 under Executive Order 14389, and the temporary 10% surcharge of 2026 ended on 24 July 2026.

Is it cheaper to import clothing from Vietnam, India or Bangladesh than from China?

On duty, yes: China pays the list 4A 7.5% that the others don't, and its 12.5% layer is also higher than India's and Bangladesh's 10%. On a $3.20 cotton T-shirt, China's duty is $1.17 against $0.93 from Vietnam and $0.85 from India or Bangladesh.
