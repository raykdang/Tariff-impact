Free tariff calculators compared: which include Section 301 and Section 232?

We read eight free US tariff calculators, ours among them, on their own pages on 25 November 2026. Five work out Section 301 and Section 232 duties for you from an HTS code or a product: Tariffs Tool, Flexport, Dave Manuel, Tariffs API and AMZ Prep. One more lists both duties for a code without costing the shipment. Ours does not look up duties at all: it takes the rate you found and shows what it does to your price, margin and break-even.

Short answer. If you want a free calculator that finds the Section 301 and Section 232 duties for your product and adds them up, the five named above all say they do, and none of their pages asked us to sign up for a basic result (Tariffs API asks for an email for the full line-by-line breakdown). They differ most in how fresh their data is and in whether they cost the shipment or only the duty. None of the five pages we read mentions margin or a break-even price.

Which free calculators include both Section 301 and Section 232?

On their own pages, five free calculators say they work out both and add them to the ordinary duty for you:

  • Tariffs Tool: by HTS code, with the country-wide duty, Section 301, Section 232 and fees stacked automatically.
  • Flexport Tariff Simulator: by HTS code or product description, with Section 232 worked out from the steel or aluminium share you declare.
  • Dave Manuel's U.S. Tariff Calculator: by HTS code or product name, stepping through each layer in order.
  • Tariffs API: by HTS code, adding every Section 301, 232 and 338 measure it holds as in force; the full line-by-line breakdown needs a free email sign-in.
  • AMZ Prep: you type the ordinary duty for your code and pick the country; it adds Section 301 by China list and Section 232 by sector.

A & A Customs Brokers' Tariff Impact Checker also lists the Section 301 and Section 232 duties that apply to a code, but it does not cost the shipment. NerdWallet's calculator and ours take a rate you already have.

Looking for a tariffstool.com alternative?

It depends on what you want from it:

  • The same kind of HTS-code duty stack: Flexport, Dave Manuel and Tariffs API work the same way. On the day we read them, Dave Manuel and Tariffs API gave the more recent data dates.
  • Many codes at once: Flexport's page offers a Pro version, through a Flexport client account, with bulk upload; Tariffs API sells a paid API with a CSV export tied to its free account.
  • What the duty does to your price and profit: none of the duty calculators above mentions margin or break-even on its page. Take the total rate from one of them and enter it in our calculator, which shows landed cost per unit, margin, monthly profit and the break-even price, and compares passing the tariff on with absorbing it.
  • Selling on Amazon: see the Amazon FBA tariff calculator.

What to check in any tariff calculator

  1. The data date. Look for when the rates were last checked, and whether a change since then touches your product.
  2. Which layers it adds. A full US duty can be the ordinary duty plus a country-wide duty, older China Section 301 duties and Section 232, and some layers replace others rather than add. See what Section 232 is.
  3. Whether it takes your exact code. Rates differ between 10-digit codes in the same chapter. How to find your HTS code shows the steps.
  4. Fees. The merchandise processing fee and, for ocean freight, the harbor maintenance fee come on top of duty. See tariff vs duty vs customs fees.
  5. What it leaves out. Several of these pages say they do not include anti-dumping or countervailing duties. None is a customs ruling.

Questions

Is there a free tariff calculator that includes Section 301 and Section 232 duties?

Yes. On 25 November 2026, Tariffs Tool, Flexport's Tariff Simulator, Dave Manuel's U.S. Tariff Calculator, Tariffs API and AMZ Prep each said on their own page that they add Section 301 and Section 232 duties for free. Tariffs API asks for an email for the full breakdown.

What are the alternatives to tariffstool.com?

Free calculators that also stack US duties by HTS code include Flexport's Tariff Simulator, Dave Manuel's U.S. Tariff Calculator and Tariffs API. For the effect of a duty on price, margin and break-even, use a cost calculator such as the Tariff Impact Calculator.

Which free tariff calculator has the newest data?

Of those we read on 25 November 2026, Tariffs API gave rates as of 25 November 2026 and Dave Manuel rules as of 22 November 2026. The others gave October 2026 dates or none.

Does the Tariff Impact Calculator include Section 301 and Section 232?

Not by itself. It takes one tariff rate: picking a country pair fills in the US country-wide duty, and you add your product's ordinary duty and any Section 301 or Section 232 duty. It then shows landed cost, margin, monthly profit and break-even price.
